## Part 1 — Who You're Selling To

### The Decision-Making Chain

Understanding who signs the cheque vs. who actually cares is the most important thing in B2B EdTech sales.

| Person | Title | What They Care About | How to Reach Them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Champion** | TPO (Training & Placement Officer) | Their own workload, placement % going up, looking good to Principal | LinkedIn, cold email, WhatsApp |
| **Influencer** | HOD / Dept. Head | Student outcomes, faculty workload | Principal's referral or direct |
| **Decision Maker** | Principal / Director | NAAC/NBA rating, college reputation, fee justification | Only after TPO buys in |
| **Blocker** | IT Head / Registrar | Data security, hosting, student data | Address proactively in demo |

**The golden rule: sell to the TPO first. They carry it upstairs.**

A TPO who believes in your product will walk into the Principal's office and say "Sir, this will improve our placement % and our NAAC score." You cannot get that from a cold email to the Principal.

These are the 3 sentences that get you a meeting. Use them in every email, call, and LinkedIn message.

> "Orbivex auto-generates your placement report — branch-wise placement %, average package, highest package — formatted exactly how NAAC expects it. One click, not two weeks of Excel."

> "Your TPO currently manages 400+ students across WhatsApp, Excel sheets, and phone calls before every drive. Orbivex handles drive scheduling, student eligibility, shortlist tracking, and offer letters — all in one place."

## Part 3 — Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1 — First 10 Colleges (Months 1–2)

**Goal:** Get 2–3 paying pilots. Don't chase scale yet. One good reference college is worth 10 cold conversations.

**Target profile:**
- Tier 2/3 engineering colleges, 300–1500 students
- Affiliated to JNTU, Osmania, VTU, Anna University, or similar
- Has a dedicated TPO (not just a faculty member doing it part-time)
- Placement % between 30–60% (enough pain, but not already solved)

**How to find them:**
- LinkedIn: search "Training and Placement Officer" + your target state
- Google: "TPO engineering college [city]" → college websites have TPO contact pages
- AICTE website: filter colleges by state, get list, then find TPOs on LinkedIn
- Ask any engineering graduate you know: "Who was your TPO? Can you introduce me?"

**Outreach sequence (per TPO):**

Day 1 — LinkedIn connection request (no message, just connect)

Day 3 — LinkedIn DM:
> "Hi [Name], I noticed you're the TPO at [College]. I've built a placement management platform specifically for engineering colleges — handles drive scheduling, student shortlists, offer tracking, and auto-generates NAAC placement reports. Would you be open to a 20-minute demo call this week?"

Day 7 — Follow-up DM if no reply:
> "Hi [Name], just following up on my earlier message. Happy to show you a live demo — takes only 20 minutes and I think the placement report feature alone will save your team 2–3 days before every NAAC cycle. Let me know a time that works."

Day 10 — Cold email to college official ID (find on college website):
> Subject: "Placement management platform for [College Name] — free pilot"
> 
> Body: "Dear [Name], I'm the founder of Orbivex, a placement and learning management platform built for engineering colleges. We help TPOs manage company drives, track student placement readiness, and auto-generate NAAC-ready reports. I'd like to offer [College Name] a free 3-month pilot — no payment, no commitment. Would you have 20 minutes this week for a demo?"

Day 14 — If still no reply, move on. Don't chase more than 3 touchpoints.

### Phase 2 — Referral Loop (Months 3–6)

Once you have 2–3 pilot colleges, the best leads come from referrals.

After a college has been using the platform for 4–6 weeks, ask:
> "Are there any other TPOs in your network who face the same placement challenges? I'd love an introduction — happy to offer them the same pilot terms."

TPOs talk to each other at university-level placement coordination meetings. One warm referral is worth 20 cold DMs.

### Phase 3 — Events & Associations (Months 4–8)

- **AICTE Faculty Development Programs** — TPOs attend these; sponsoring or presenting costs very little
- **University-level Placement Coordinator meetings** — JNTU, VTU, Anna University run these; getting on the agenda for 10 minutes is powerful
- **LinkedIn content** — Post weekly about placement challenges, NAAC preparation tips, engineering college placement data. This builds inbound interest from TPOs who find you organically.

## Part 4 — The Demo Script

**Duration:** 20–25 minutes  
**Format:** Screen share, live demo on your seeded demo instance  
**Audience:** TPO (primary), sometimes HOD or Principal sits in

### Minute 0–3: Start with their pain, not your product
Ask before showing anything:
- "How many drives did you conduct last year?"
- "How do you currently manage the shortlist after each round?"
- "What does your placement report process look like before NAAC?"

Let them talk. The more they describe their Excel + WhatsApp chaos, the more your product is the obvious solution.

### Minute 3–8: The TPO Dashboard
Show the TPO Dashboard. Walk them through:
- Total students, placement %, upcoming drives at a glance
- "This is what you see every morning instead of opening 4 Excel files"

### Minute 8–14: Schedule a Drive (live)
Create a drive in front of them:
- Company name, date, eligibility criteria (CGPA, batch year)
- Click "Auto-add eligible students" — show 200 students added in 2 seconds
- Show the notification that fires to all eligible students
- "Your students just got notified. No WhatsApp broadcast needed."

Walk through shortlisting after a round — click shortlisted / eliminated next to student names.

### Minute 14–18: The Report (this is your closer)
Go to Placement Analytics. Show:
- Branch-wise placement % bar chart
- Top recruiting companies
- Avg and highest package
- Then click "Download PDF Report"

Say: **"This is what you submit to NAAC. It takes one click. Right now it probably takes you two weeks."**

Watch their face. This is usually the moment they lean forward.

### Minute 18–22: Student Side
Briefly show the student view — Company Prep roadmaps, placement readiness score, LSRW module.
- "Your students see their readiness score. They know what to work on before TCS comes."
- "You can see which students are below 50% readiness and call them in for coaching."

### Minute 22–25: Close
Don't end with "what do you think?" — end with a specific next step:
> "I'd like to offer you a free 3-month pilot — your whole college, no cost. If you see value, we talk about pricing. If not, no hard feelings. Can we set up a call with your Principal this week to get the pilot started?"

## Part 5 — Pricing Model

### Recommended Structure

**Pilot:** Free for 3 months (first college in a city/district). This removes all friction.

**After pilot — Annual subscription:**

| Tier | Students | Price (INR/year) | What's included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Up to 500 | ₹75,000 | All features, 1 college, email support |
| **Growth** | 501–1500 | ₹1,50,000 | All features, multi-branch, WhatsApp support |
| **Campus** | 1500–4000+ | ₹2,50,000 | All features, multi-college group, dedicated onboarding |

**Why this works:**
- ₹75,000/year for 500 students = ₹150/student/year. A college charges ₹1–2 lakh/year per student. This is 0.1% of what they earn from one student. No CFO can object.
- Group/university pricing: if a university system (e.g., 5 colleges under one trust) signs, offer 30% discount for the group. One sale = 5 implementations.

### What to avoid
- Per-feature pricing at this stage (too complex to sell)
- Monthly billing (colleges work on annual budgets approved in April–May)
- Charging per user login (creates internal resistance from IT admins)

## Part 6 — Competitive Positioning

### Main competitors TPOs will mention

**Superset** (superset.ai)
- Strong brand, well-funded
- Primarily a recruiter-facing platform (companies post, students apply)
- Weakness: No learning/prep content, no AI agents, no LSRW, expensive
- Your angle: "Superset helps you post jobs. Orbivex helps your students get them."

**HirePro / Mettl** (now Mercer)
- Focused on online assessments and proctoring
- Weakness: No placement management, no learning path, very expensive
- Your angle: "HirePro tests students. Orbivex prepares them and tracks the whole placement lifecycle."

**Campus Credentials / CoCubes**
- Older platforms, very basic UI
- Weakness: Not updated for modern workflows, no AI features
- Your angle: "Built in 2026, not 2012. Has AI quiz generation, company-specific prep, and placement analytics."

**In-house Excel + Google Forms (most common)**
- This is what 80% of Tier 2/3 colleges actually use
- Your angle: "This is free, so you're technically not replacing anything. You're adding a system where none exists."

### Your unfair advantages
1. **Built-in managed AI** — most SaaS EdTech platforms have no AI or resell expensive third-party APIs and pass the cost on. Orbivex runs its own managed AI engine, so quiz generation, course generation, AI tutor, and smart grading are included in the subscription at no per-request cost to the college.
2. **NAAC report** — no competitor has a one-click NAAC placement report. This is a genuine gap.
3. **Built for Indian context** — LSRW (critical for tier 2/3 students), company-specific prep for TCS/Infosys/Wipro patterns, hall ticket numbers, enrollment numbers, branch/section hierarchy.
4. **Price** — you can be 50–70% cheaper than Superset or HirePro and still be profitable, because your infrastructure cost is low.

## Part 7 — Objections and How to Handle Them

**"We already have a placement portal from our university."**
> "Most university portals handle job postings and resume uploads. We handle the actual TPO workflow — drive management, round tracking, shortlists, offer letters, and the placement report. They're complementary, not competing."

**"Our students don't use apps / portals."**
> "That's exactly what makes this different — we send WhatsApp notifications [coming soon] and SMS, so students don't have to log in proactively. The content comes to them."

**"We don't have budget this year."**
> "That's fine — the pilot is completely free for 3 months, no purchase order needed. If you see value, we can discuss including it in next year's budget. Most of our pilot colleges get management approval based on the NAAC report alone."

**"What about data security? Where is student data stored?"**
> "All data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS in the Mumbai region for Indian data residency). We don't share any student data with third parties. You can sign a data processing agreement if your IT team needs one."

**"What if you shut down?"**
> "Fair question. We can export all your data — students, drives, offers, reports — as CSV/Excel at any time. You're never locked in."

## Part 8 — Development Modules Roadmap

These are the features to build next, in order of sales impact.

### Q3 2026 — Immediate (High Sales Impact)

**1. WhatsApp Notifications (2 weeks)**
- Use Twilio WhatsApp Business API or Interakt
- Drive announcements, shortlist results, offer notifications
- Single biggest adoption driver for students

**2. Resume Builder (3 weeks)**
- Form-based: personal details, education, skills, projects, certifications
- Auto-pulls quiz scores, courses completed, LSRW scores from the platform
- Exports as PDF (use html2pdf.js or Puppeteer)
- This is what students will use daily and show to friends → viral growth

**3. Student Attendance in Drives**
- QR code check-in or manual mark present/absent on drive day
- Simple but TPOs love it — replaces paper sheets

**4. Bulk Offer Letter Upload**
- TPO uploads Excel of selected students → all get notified at once
- Currently they do this one by one

### Q4 2026 — Growth Features

**5. Recruiter Portal (4 weeks)**
- Companies get their own login to post drives, filter students, download shortlists
- Makes your platform attractive from both sides (college + company)
- Allows companies to directly schedule drives without going through TPO email

**6. Alumni Tracking (2 weeks)**
- After graduation, students become alumni
- Track "where are they now" — company, role, package
- NAAC needs alumni outcome data; currently no college tracks this well

**7. Mock Interview Module (4 weeks)**
- Student records a 2-minute video answer to a question
- AI transcribes + scores on relevance, communication, confidence
- Very high perceived value, drives daily usage

**8. Skill Certification Badges (1 week)**
- After completing a course + passing the quiz with >70%, student gets a digital badge
- Shareable on LinkedIn
- Free marketing for Orbivex every time a student posts their badge

### Q1 2027 — Monetization Multipliers

**9. Job Board with Company Subscriptions**
- Companies pay ₹5,000–15,000/month to post jobs directly to your student network
- As you scale to 10+ colleges (4000+ students), this becomes a real revenue stream

**10. Multi-College Group Dashboard**
- University or trust management can see aggregated placement stats across all their colleges
- This is the enterprise upsell — one group = 5–8 colleges paying

**11. AI Interview Prep Bot**
- Chat-based mock interviewer for specific companies (TCS, Infosys, etc.)
- Uses your existing AI infrastructure
- Premium feature for Growth/Campus tier

**12. Parent Portal**
- Parents can see their child's placement readiness score and enrolled drives
- Unusual but very effective for Indian college context — parents are highly involved
- Drives renewals because parents ask the college to continue the subscription

## Part 9 — First 90 Days Action Plan

| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1–2 | Set up a clean demo instance with 50 fake students, 3 drives, sample offers. Test the full TPO workflow yourself. |
| 3–4 | Identify 20 target TPOs on LinkedIn (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai). Send connection requests. |
| 5–6 | Start DM outreach to first 20. Goal: 5 demo calls booked. |
| 7–8 | Run demo calls. Target: 2 pilot agreements. |
| 9–10 | Onboard pilot colleges. Be their WhatsApp contact personally — respond within 2 hours. |
| 11–12 | After 4 weeks of usage, ask for a testimonial and a referral. |

### The most important thing

The hardest part of selling to colleges is not the product — it's getting in the door. One warm introduction from someone the TPO trusts (a mutual faculty contact, a former student, a university official) is worth 3 months of cold outreach. Use every connection you have to get a first meeting.

Once you get the first meeting, the product sells itself. The NAAC report demo alone has closed every conversation we've stress-tested it in.
